Privacy Policy — BabyHabit

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what data BabyHabit ("the app") collects, how it is used, and how you can control it. BabyHabit is a parenting app to help you track your child's daily care, developmental milestones, mood, and growth activities. It is designed to be used by parents/caregivers, not directly by children.

1. Data we collect

Account data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored as a hash — we never store or see your plain-text password)
  • Name
  • Locale (language preference)
  • Avatar photo, if you choose to upload one (via your device's camera or photo library)

Child profile data

For each child profile you create:

  • Name
  • Birth date
  • Gender
  • Temperament quiz results
  • Avatar photo, if you choose to upload one

Daily tracking data you log

  • Daily care entries (feeding, sleep, diaper, activity logs)
  • Mood/emotion journal entries
  • Developmental milestone records (CDC-reference based)
  • Socratic Play activity progress and your written notes

Family sharing

If you invite another caregiver to your child's "Care Circle," their email and role are stored to manage shared access.

Device permissions

  • Camera / Photo Library — only used when you choose to set a profile avatar. Photos are not accessed otherwise.
  • Microphone — only used if you record voice notes within the app. Voice recordings are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or any third party.
  • Notifications — used to send you local reminders (e.g. daily care reminders). We do not register your device for push notifications from a server.

Analytics and crash reporting

  • We use PostHog to understand feature usage (e.g. which screens are viewed, which features are used, session length). This is off by default and only active if you opt in via Settings. We do not send your name, email, or child's data to PostHog — only anonymous usage events tied to a random session ID.
  • We use Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and error diagnostics so we can fix bugs. This always runs (not tied to the analytics opt-in toggle) and does not include your name, email, or child's personal data — only technical error data (stack traces, device/OS info).

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use any third-party advertising SDK.
  • We do not use cross-app tracking (no Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because we don't track you across other apps or websites).
  • We do not send your voice recordings anywhere — they stay on your device.
  • We do not use any AI/LLM service to process your child's data.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the app's core features (tracking, reminders, milestone progress, activity suggestions).
  • To let you share access with trusted caregivers (Care Circle), if you choose to.
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems (crash reports).
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns (only if you opt in).

4. Data storage and security

Your account and child data are stored on our servers, protected by authentication (password hashing, token-based API access). Data you enter locally (before syncing) is also cached on your device.

5. Your rights

  • Account deletion: You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This permanently removes your account and associated child profiles and data from our servers.
  • Analytics opt-out: You can turn off usage analytics at any time from Settings.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask questions about this policy by contacting us (see Contact below).

6. Children's privacy

BabyHabit is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. Child profile data (name, birth date, etc.) is entered and controlled by the parent/caregiver account holder.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@babyhabit.app
